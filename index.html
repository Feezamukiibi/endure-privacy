Endure privacy policy

Effective 9 November 2026

Endure is a running app that tracks your runs and gives voice directions along a route. This page explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

Information the app uses

Location. While a run is in progress, Endure uses your device's precise location to measure distance, pace and elevation, to show your position on the map, and to give turn-by-turn voice directions. On Android, the app shows a notification while it tracks your run, so tracking continues when the screen is locked. Tracking stops when you finish or discard the run.

Run history and profile. Your finished runs (route, distance, time, pace, elevation, calories) and any profile details you enter (weight, height, age, sex, weekly goal) are stored only on your device. They are used to calculate calorie estimates and show your statistics.

What leaves your device

Route planning. When you tap points on the map to plan a route, the coordinates of those points are sent to a routing service to find walkable paths between them. This is the OpenStreetMap community routing service, or Mapbox if the app is configured to use it. Your name or any account details are not sent.

Maps. Map tiles are loaded from OpenFreeMap, a free public service built on OpenStreetMap data. To show the part of the map you are looking at, your device requests tiles for that area, which means the service can see your IP address and the area requested. No account or identifier is sent.

Voice directions. Spoken directions use the text-to-speech engine already on your device.

What we do not do

Endure has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics. We do not sell your information, and we do not receive your run history or location on any server of ours.

Your choices

You can deny or withdraw location permission in your device settings, but the app cannot track runs without it. You can delete any run or saved route inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes all data it stored on your device.

Children

Endure is not directed at children under 13.
