Endure privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026

Endure is a running app that tracks your runs, creates routes, and gives voice directions along a route. This page explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

Information the app uses

Location. While a run is in progress, Endure uses your device's precise location to measure distance, pace and elevation, to show your position on the map, and to give turn-by-turn voice directions. On Android, the app shows a notification while it tracks your run, so tracking continues when you switch apps or lock the screen. Tracking stops when you finish or discard the run. You can also choose to use your current location as the start when creating a route.

Run history, goals and profile. Your finished runs (route, distance, time, pace, elevation, calories), your saved routes, your goals and race plans, and any profile details you enter (weight, height, age, sex, running level and reasons for running) are stored only on your device. They are used to calculate calorie estimates, suggest goals and show your statistics.

What leaves your device

Route planning and creation. When you draw a route or ask Endure to create one, the coordinates of the route points are sent to a routing service to find walkable paths between them. For a created route these include the start you chose, which may be your current location, and points around it that the app picks. This is the OpenStreetMap community routing service. Your name or any account details are not sent.

Maps. Map tiles are loaded from OpenFreeMap, a free public service built on OpenStreetMap data. To show the part of the map you are looking at, your device requests tiles for that area, which means the service can see your IP address and the area requested. No account or identifier is sent.

Terrain. When you create or save a route, the app requests elevation map tiles for the surrounding area from Terrain Tiles, a public dataset hosted on Amazon Web Services, to work out how much climbing the route has. The request reveals the area and your IP address. No account or identifier is sent.

Voice directions. Spoken directions use the text-to-speech engine already on your device.

What we do not do

Endure has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics. We do not sell your information, and we do not receive your run history or location on any server of ours.

Your choices

You can deny or withdraw location permission in your device settings, but the app cannot track runs without it. You can deny the notification permission, but then the tracking notification will not be shown. You can delete any run, saved route or goal inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes all data it stored on your device.

Children

Endure is not directed at children under 13.
